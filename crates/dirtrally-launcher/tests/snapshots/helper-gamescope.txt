# linux/dirtrally-play --play on a 3840x2160 screen, gamescope installed

## Recommended (first run)
starting at 3840x2160, scaler linear
SDL_GAMECONTROLLER_ALLOW_BACKGROUND_EVENTS=1 SDL_JOYSTICK_ALLOW_BACKGROUND_EVENTS=1 REA_INNER_MODE=native REA_GAME_ENV_FILE=<home>/.config/dirtrally-launcher/game.env REA_GAME_PROCS= REA_GAME_SETTLE=3 REA_TEST=1 gamescope -W 3840 -H 2160 -w 3840 -h 2160 -f -S fit -F linear -- sh <home>/.config/dirtrally-launcher/inner.sh <home>/DiRTRallyLauncher --no-window
